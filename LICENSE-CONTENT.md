In short: you may copy, redistribute, and adapt this content for any
purpose, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit
to ChemistryLab OS, link to the license, and indicate if changes were

## Why two licenses

Software and educational content have different reuse norms — a school
adapting a lesson isn't "modifying source code," and CC BY 4.0 is the
license teachers and curriculum authors actually expect. Splitting the
two also makes it possible to bring in appropriately-licensed third-party
data or media (see `DATA_SOURCES.md`) without accidentally relicensing
this project's own code, or vice versa.

## Third-party data and media

Do not add a dataset, image, or other asset unless its license is
verified compatible with CC BY 4.0 (or a more permissive license) and its
provenance is recorded — see `DATA_SOURCES.md`. Do not copy simulations,
images, or educational material from a project whose license is
incompatible with the terms above.
